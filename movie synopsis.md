####            **Title: The Lost Lantern**



####  **Synopsis:**

* When curious teen **Mira** discovers an ancient, glowing **lantern** in her grandfather’s attic, she learns it’s a mystical compass pointing toward hidden wonders — but only at dawn. With her **best friend Arun** and his adventurous **dog Chiku**, **Mira** sets off into the misty hills beyond their village.



* Their journey leads them through whispering forests, over sparkling streams, and into forgotten caves where shadows seem alive. Along the way they solve nature’s riddles, escape perilous traps, and uncover clues about a long-lost treasure believed to grant one true wish.



* As the sun rises on their final day, the lantern leads them to a breathtaking hidden valley where they must **choose**: claim the treasure for themselves, or protect it from those who would exploit its magic. In the end, the real treasure turns out to be the **courage, friendship, and unbreakable bond** they discover within themselves.



####  **Theme:**

* **Courage, friendship, and the magic of Discovering.**
